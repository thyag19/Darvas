# Draft data-permission requests (for you to review, adapt and send; nothing has been sent)

Send from the entity that will operate the site. Record each reply in `config/sources.json` (`clearance_evidence`, `display_evidence`,
`terms_checked_on`) only when it is in writing. A reply that is silent on a point does not grant it.

**Common questions for every source**
1. May we retrieve this data automatically (method, endpoint, frequency; we propose one request per source per hour with backoff)?
2. May we store it and keep a history (how long)?
3. May we display it publicly on our website, as end-of-day / delayed values, with attribution? Any required wording or delay?
4. May we publish values derived from it (yields, premium to NAV, income vs capital split)?
5. May users download a CSV of what is displayed?
6. Fees, if any; permitted commercial use; restrictions on redistribution; termination terms.

**NSE Data & Analytics / BSE**: end-of-day prices and corporate-action (distribution) records for listed REITs, InvITs and ETFs.
The reported terms require written permission for copying, storing or redistributing content and bar automated collection (see Phase 2 report).
**AMFI**: daily NAVs for ETFs. Reported terms cover personal, non-commercial use; ask what agreement covers a public commercial site.
**Index providers (NSE Indices, BSE Indices)**: index levels for tracking difference; separate terms apply.
**Issuers / AMCs (per trust or fund)**: permission to transcribe disclosed distributions, NAV, leverage and holdings with attribution.
